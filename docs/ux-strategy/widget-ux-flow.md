# Widget UX Flow

## Objective
The widget should feel like an intelligent sales representative, not a blank chatbot.

## First open state
- Show a confident welcome message.
- Explain that the widget already understands the website.
- Present 4–6 clickable suggestions immediately.
- Use high-signal actions such as pricing, products, services, and book demo.

## Conversation behavior
- Reply with grounded, business-aware answers.
- Offer next-step actions after each answer.
- Guide toward product discovery, lead qualification, and demo booking.

## Design principles
- Never present an empty chat window.
- Use clear, helpful chips rather than vague prompts.
- Keep microcopy concise and outcome-focused.

## Why this improves conversion
A strong first interaction reduces friction and increases perceived intelligence. Visitors who see useful suggestions are more likely to continue and take action.
