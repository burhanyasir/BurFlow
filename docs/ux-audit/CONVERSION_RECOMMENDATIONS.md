# Conversion Recommendations — BurFlow

## Core Conversion Goal
The product should make the visitor feel that BurFlow is already understanding their business and helping them make the right decision.

## Hero Section Recommendations
- Make the primary CTA the most visually dominant element
- Keep the opening promise tight and outcome-based
- Reduce the number of headline variations and competing calls to action
- Put trust proof in the hero or directly beneath it

## Scan Flow Recommendations
- Frame the scan as a business acceleration experience, not a technical workflow
- Explain the value of the scan before the visitor acts
- Show what the visitor will receive after scanning
- Reduce friction by making the scan feel immediate and low-risk

## CTA Recommendations
### High-performing options
- Scan My Website
- Book a Demo
- See How It Works
- Start Free

### Avoid
- Learn More
- Get Started
- Contact Us
- Try It Now

## Widget Interaction Recommendations
- Guide the visitor toward a concrete next step after every response
- Use contextual actions rather than generic ones
- Avoid ending the conversation with an open-ended prompt unless it is highly relevant
- Present product and pricing guidance as helpful recommendations

## Pricing Recommendations
- Present pricing as business value, not feature count
- Anchor the pricing section around ROI, speed, and conversion impact
- Add comparison and plan guidance inside the widget

## Onboarding Recommendations
- Make initial onboarding feel like a guided launch, not a setup burden
- Show the visitor what the system will understand and how it will help
- Keep the path from signup to value short and obvious

## Conversion Killers to Remove
- Unclear primary action
- Too much generic copy
- Weak proof near the hero
- Weak differentiation between BurFlow and generic bots
- Lack of persuasive next-step guidance inside the widget
