# Trust Review — BurFlow

## Trust Objectives
To convert visitors, BurFlow must make them feel that:
- the system is credible
- it understands their business
- it is safe to engage
- it can help them make a good decision

## Recommended Trust Placement
### Above the fold
- customer logos
- short testimonials
- one or two statistics
- clear privacy reassurance

### Near the hero CTA
- “No training on your data” messaging
- “Live in under 10 minutes” reassurance
- simple security/privacy message

### Near the widget
- “Guided by your website context” messaging
- clear mention of grounded answers and business context

### Near pricing and signup
- guarantee messaging
- integration badges
- customer outcome stories

## Recommended Trust Assets
- customer logo strip
- 2–3 trusted testimonials
- one strong statistic related to outcomes
- security/privacy messaging
- optional integration badges
- a plain-language guarantee

## Trust Gaps to Address
- The current page does not yet fully establish credibility early enough
- The widget should signal that it is grounded in the company’s known offer, not just generic AI
- The product should communicate business safety and clarity as strongly as it communicates capability
