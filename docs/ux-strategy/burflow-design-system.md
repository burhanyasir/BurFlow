# BurFlow Design System

## Product personality
Premium, calm, confident, and intelligent.

## Tone of voice
- Clear
- Specific
- Commercially grounded
- Confident without sounding arrogant

## Visual language
- Strong contrast
- Premium spacing
- Clean UI hierarchy
- Minimal but meaningful motion

## Core principles
- Emphasize outcomes over features.
- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep the experience calm and focused.
- Treat the browser widget as a sales touchpoint, not a gimmick.

## Recommended UI patterns
- Large primary CTA
- Short proof strip near hero
- Visual workflow with 3–4 steps
- Widget chips with obvious commercial intent
- High-trust microcopy and minimal friction

## Why this improves conversion
A disciplined design system signals competence and reduces uncertainty. That makes the product feel more trustworthy and easier to adopt.
