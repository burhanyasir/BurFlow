# Competitive Analysis Report

## Companies reviewed
- SiteGPT
- Intercom
- Zendesk AI
- HubSpot
- Drift
- Salesforce Einstein
- Tidio
- Crisp
- Stripe
- Linear
- Notion
- Vercel
- Clay

## What they do well
- Strong product marketing and familiar UX patterns.
- Clear onboarding and strong brand narratives.
- Established trust signals and polished interfaces.

## What they do poorly
- Many feel like support tools or generic chatbot products.
- Several position themselves around support, not revenue conversion.
- Some introduce too much complexity or too much setup friction.

## BurFlow differentiation opportunities
- Website-first intelligence as the core value.
- Sales-agent positioning rather than support-agent positioning.
- A premium, confident experience tailored to lead conversion and demo booking.
- A cleaner, more focused journey from website scan to activation.

## Strategic takeaway
BurFlow should not try to be “another chatbot.” It should be positioned as an AI Website Sales Agent that understands the business and helps convert visitors into customers.
