# Widget UX Audit — BurFlow Sales Agent

## Executive Summary
The widget has the right foundation, but it still needs to feel more like a premium sales representative than a generic assistant. The experience should feel consultative, action-oriented, and confident from the first second a visitor opens it.

## Review Lens
Reviewed as a first-time customer, from the perspective of:
- first impression
- greeting quality
- suggestion chip usefulness
- CTA visibility
- empty-state quality
- typing and loading states
- animation quality
- sales psychology
- mobile responsiveness
- accessibility

## Current Strengths
- The widget already has a visible entry point
- The concept of a sales assistant is clear
- Suggestion actions are a good start
- The interface has a workable structure for future growth

## UX Findings

### 1. First impression is still too generic
Priority: High
The opening state should immediately feel intelligent and specific. A visitor should not feel like they are opening a blank support box.

### 2. Suggestion chips need stronger sales intent
Priority: High
The current chips should move beyond generic assistance into decision support, not just navigation.

### 3. Empty states should feel “alive”
Priority: High
When opened, the widget should demonstrate that it already understands the company and the visitor’s likely intent.

### 4. Typing and loading states should feel more premium
Priority: Medium
A simple typing indicator is not enough. The widget should create a sense of thoughtful response preparation.

### 5. Mobile experience needs a more deliberate layout
Priority: Medium
The widget should feel equally polished on mobile, with generous touch targets and a stronger sense of focus.

## Recommended Improvements
### High Priority
1. Open with a contextual greeting that references the company and the visitor journey.
2. Surface top-of-funnel sales actions immediately.
3. Make the first message feel like a recommendation, not a generic acknowledgement.
4. Use smarter suggestion chips that evolve based on conversation context.
5. Reinforce CTA visibility with more premium button treatment.

### Medium Priority
1. Add subtle animation throughout the chat lifecycle.
2. Improve streaming impression with rich state transitions.
3. Introduce a stronger visual separation between user messages, assistant guidance, and conversion actions.
4. Support richer cards for pricing, plans, demos, and FAQ.

### Low Priority
1. Add additional motion polish and micro-interactions.
2. Introduce contextual personas or tones depending on visitor intent.

## Sales Psychology Recommendations
- Lead with confidence rather than neutrality
- Frame the next step clearly
- Offer choice without overwhelming the visitor
- Keep the experience moving toward a useful outcome
- Avoid generic “Anything else?” phrasing
