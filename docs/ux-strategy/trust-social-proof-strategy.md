# Trust & Social Proof Strategy

## Trust levers
- Show the product is grounded in the customer’s own website.
- Use concrete proof points: live in under 10 minutes, no training on your data, grounded responses.
- Show that the system is designed to help with sales outcomes.

## Social proof tactics
- Customer logos.
- Short testimonials from growth and revenue teams.
- Outcome-oriented case study snapshots.
- Implementation time and deployment clarity.

## Authority signals
- Use clear product positioning language.
- Distinguish BurFlow from generic chatbot tools.
- Emphasize premium, reliable, revenue-oriented behavior.

## Why this improves conversion
Trust reduces perceived risk, and proof reduces uncertainty. Together they make the buyer more willing to try the system and invest in it.
