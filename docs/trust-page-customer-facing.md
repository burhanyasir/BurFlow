# How BurFlow Keeps Your Data Safe

Your business data stays yours. Every customer on BurFlow gets a fully separate, 
private workspace — your conversations, files, and settings are never visible to 
anyone else on the platform. We test this directly, regularly, by deliberately 
trying to make one customer's data leak into another's, and confirming it can't.

**If something goes wrong, we fail safely, not silently.** If a visitor's chat 
connection drops or something on our end hiccups, the widget shows a clear message 
and offers a next step — it never leaves your visitor staring at a blank or broken 
chat window. And if we can't scan every page of your site during setup, we don't 
leave you stuck: your widget goes live with a basic setup right away, so you're 
never blocked from launching.

**What we're actively working on:** a dedicated review process for flagging any AI 
response that didn't hit the mark, so you always have a way to tell us — and we're 
building that next.
