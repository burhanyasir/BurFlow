Frequently asked questions

What does robots.txt do?

It tells search engine crawlers which parts of your site they may and may not crawl, and points them to your sitemap.

Where do I put the file?

In your site root — https://example.com/robots.txt. Most crawlers pick up changes within 24 hours.
