### 4. FAQ Copy

**Section Title**
Frequently Asked Questions

**How does Conversation Engine prevent incorrect or hallucinated answers?**
Conversation Engine restricts responses strictly to the contents of your published Knowledge Bases. If a visitor inquiry falls outside your source material, the widget returns a custom offline fallback response rather than generating speculative answers.

**How fast can we set up and launch?**
Most teams deploy in under 10 minutes. Simply create a workspace, upload your existing PDF, text, or FAQ files, customize your widget colors, and paste the embed script on your site.

**Can we remove all Conversation Engine branding?**
Yes. Professional and Enterprise tiers include complete white-label customization. You can adjust theme colors, upload custom icons, modify messaging, and disable platform attribution completely.

**What happens if our message volume exceeds our monthly allocation?**
We track your usage transparently. You will receive automatic usage warnings at 80% and 100% capacity. You can upgrade your tier instantly from your dashboard without service disruption.

## SECTION II: Creative Direction & Interactive WebGL Brief

**Concept Title:** The Architecture of Knowledge

**Visual Style & Vibe:** Linear precision + Stripe light-fields + Vercel minimalism + Apple hardware depth + Framer fluid interactions.

**Core Aesthetic:** Matte dark surface (#050507), volumetric ray-marched lighting, frosted glass physics ($IOR = 1.52$), dynamic chromatic refraction, and 120Hz spring physics.

### 1. Hero Animation & WebGL Core

**The 3D Knowledge Monolith**
- **Initial View:** As the page loads, the hero headline "Instant Support Driven by Your Knowledge Base." floats over a dark background. At the center of the viewport rests a floating, translucent 3D Knowledge Core rendered in WebGL via Three.js.
- **Interactive Particle Swarm:** Thousands of instanced micro-particles (representing unstructured data, documents, and web URLs) drift in subtle turbulence around the core.

**Interaction:**
- As the user moves their cursor across the viewport, the core rotates using smooth inertia dampening (dampEa(0.05)).
- Hovering directly over the core triggers a subtle refraction shift—light bends through the glass geometry using custom GLSL shaders with chromatic dispersion.
- **Sub-Headline Visual Trigger:** Mentioning "10-minute embed deployment" triggers a fast, tactile pulse across the node mesh, forming a glowing circuit pattern that radiates outward toward the Start Free Trial call-to-action button.

```
[ Hero Text: "Instant Support Driven by Your Knowledge Base." ]
                          |
             ( Interactive WebGL Node Core )
             /             |             \
 [ Raw Data Streams ] -> [ Neural Refractor ] -> [ Structured Answers ]
```

### 2. Scroll Animations & Narrative Flow

**Spatial Depth & Camera Rigging**
- **Parallax Axis Mapping:** Scroll depth is tied directly to Three.js camera position (camera.position.z and camera.position.y).
- **Section 1 to Section 2 Transition (Hero to Features):** As the user scrolls down, the hero 3D Knowledge Core slowly explodes along its exploded-view axes, unfolding into six glowing glass panels that slide into place behind the Feature Grid.

**Feature Reveal Mechanics:**
- **Knowledge-Aware Intelligence:** A 3D document stack unfolds vertically; glowing beam vectors connect lines of text to a central illuminated node (symbolizing zero-hallucination grounding).
- **Pure White-Label Architecture:** A glass frame rotates 180 degrees on the Y-axis, instantly stripping away platform branding to reveal custom color highlights and logo slots.
- **Data Sovereignty & Security:** A floating metallic shield mesh wraps around the data core, forming an impenetrable, reflective barrier that repels stray ambient particles.

## SECTION III: Comprehensive Brand Visual & Illustration System

**Design Philosophy:** The Materiality of Knowledge

**Core Visual Language:** Precision engineering meets fluid intelligence. The aesthetic merges the tactile clarity of Apple hardware, the vibrant light-fields of Stripe, and the structural rigor of Vercel and Linear.

### 1. Primary Design Primitives & Material Properties

#### A. Glass Dynamics & Refraction

- **Material Specification:** Ultra-thin frosted sapphire glass ($IOR = 1.52$, Transmission $= 0.95$).
- **Surface Finish:** Micro-roughness ($0.04$) to catch directional highlight rays without obscuring underlying content.
- **Edge Treatments:** Multi-pass chamfered edges with simulated chromatic dispersion ($RGB$ wavelength offset: $\pm 2.4\text{px}$). Edges catch ambient lighting to create crisp, glowing outlines.

#### B. Color Palette & Dynamic Gradients

- **Base Surface:** Deep Void (#050507) and Obsidian (#0B0C10).
- **Primary Spectral Halos:**
  - Hyper-Indigo: #5865F2 → #0052FF
  - Precision Cyan: #00F0FF → #00A3FF
  - Neural Amber: #FFB800 → #FF5500
- **Mesh Gradient Structure:** 4-point dynamic GLSL mesh gradients with continuous noise-based wave distortion ($Simplex\ 3D$, frequency: $0.15$).

#### C. Abstract Shapes & Geometry

- **Primitives:** Precision hyper-toruses, truncated polyhedrons, extruded filleted rounded rectangles ($R = 24\text{px}$), and intersecting vector ribbons.
- **Volumetric Topology:** Objects feature subtle surface grooves and etched circuit tracks that channel glowing light pulses.

### 2. Iconography & Particle System Architecture

#### A. Icon Style Guidelines

- **Grid:** Built on a $24 \times 24\text{px}$ grid with a $2\text{px}$ stroke default.
- **Dual-Layer Aesthetics:**
  - **Foreground:** High-contrast vector strokes in pure white (#FFFFFF) or high-visibility blue.
  - **Background Backdrop:** Frosted semi-transparent glass badges with $8\text{px}$ backdrop blur (backdrop-filter: blur(8px)).
- **Corner Radii:** Continuous curvature splines ($Squircle\ Smoothness = 100\%$).

#### B. Particle System (The "Data Swarm")

- **Behavioral Physics:** Instanced GPU particles governed by vector force fields and Perlin noise turbulence.
- **Visual States:**
  - **Unstructured State:** Scattered ambient dust particles drifting loosely.
  - **Structured State:** Particles snapping along magnetic grid lines to form geometric structures (symbolizing knowledge ingestion and processing).
- **Render Properties:** Additive blend mode (THREE.AdditiveBlending), point size range: $1.5\text{px} - 4.0\text{px}$ with distance attenuation.

### 3. Holographic & AI Imagery Style

#### A. Holographic Shader FX

- **Interferometry Sheen:** Thin-film interference shaders layered over 3D geometry, producing shifting violet-to-cyan iridescence based on viewing angle.
- **Scanline Dynamics:** Subdued horizontal raster lines ($1\text{px}$ line, $3\text{px}$ spacing, opacity $8\%$) moving vertically across holographic UI projections.

#### B. AI Imagery & Imagery Direction

- **Visual Metaphor:** Never depict generic AI brains or robots. Instead, portray AI as luminescent physical structures—architectural light lattices, crystalline refraction blocks, and self-assembling geometric matrices.
- **Lighting Environment:** High-contrast studio lighting with dual rim-lights (Cyan Key Light, Deep Violet Fill Light) set against obsidian backdrops.

### 4. Touchpoint Specific Illustrations

#### A. Dashboard Illustrations

- **Analytics Node Lattice:** A 3D isometric view of an elevated glass platform displaying floating bar graphs and glowing data nodes connected by luminous light threads, visualizing real-time conversation volume and token metrics.
- **Multi-Tenant Layer Stack:** Overlapping, floating translucent glass sheets floating in 3D space, each representing an isolated workspace or client tier with distinct brand accents.

- **Add Knowledge:** Multi-format document cards (PDF, DOCX, Web links) transforming into glowing particle streams that channel into a central core.
- **Review Knowledge:** A vertical laser ring passing over an indexed cube, illuminating clean data structures and vector embeddings.
- **Configure Widget:** A 3D spatial viewport showing floating alignment guidelines and theme sliders surrounding a crisp widget outline.
- **Install & Celebrate:** A glowing 3D code bracket exploding into geometric light shards and golden particle bursts ("Setup Complete!").
