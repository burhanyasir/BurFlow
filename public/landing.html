Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to launch a client?

With our website scanner and one-click deploy, most clients are live in under 15 minutes — from first call to installed widget.

Do I need to write the bot's content?

No. The website scanner reads the client's existing site to extract services, FAQs, and team info. You review and approve, then deploy.

Can each client have their own branding?

Yes. Every tenant has its own colors, logo, greeting, subdomain, and optional custom domain.

Which industries are supported?

Dental, restaurants, law firms, gyms, real estate, and salons ship with templates today, with more added continuously.

Is it really 24/7?

Yes. The assistant runs on our infrastructure with health monitoring and automatic recovery.

How are appointments booked?

The bot collects service, date, time, name, and contact, then confirms — exactly like a front-desk booking.
