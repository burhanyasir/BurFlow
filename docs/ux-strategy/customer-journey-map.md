# Customer Journey Map

## Stage 1: Awareness
Visitor arrives from search, social, or a referral.

- What they think: “This looks relevant, but I need to know if it’s real.”
- Main concern: Will this be another AI gimmick?
- What they need: clear promise, proof, and a low-risk next step.
- Trust builder: strong hero claim, visual confidence, immediate evidence.

## Stage 2: Interest
Visitor explores the homepage.

- What they think: “This could help, but I need to understand how.”
- Main concern: Will it work for my specific business?
- What they need: simple explanation and credible evidence.
- Trust builder: workflow steps, specific examples, and proof of website scanning.

## Stage 3: Evaluation
Visitor compares BurFlow with alternatives.

- What they think: “Can this replace or complement my existing setup?”
- Main concern: integration, quality, and trust.
- What they need: differentiation, positioning, and proof points.
- Trust builder: clear comparison, transparent setup, security and grounding language.

## Stage 4: Activation
Visitor is ready to try the product.

- What they think: “I want to see it on my site.”
- Main concern: friction and time-to-value.
- What they need: a single clear action and straightforward setup.
- Trust builder: scan-my-website CTA, demo widget, guided onboarding.

## Stage 5: Conversion
Visitor becomes a customer or signs up.

- What they think: “This feels credible enough to invest in.”
- Main concern: ROI and operational fit.
- What they need: confidence, clarity, and reassurance.
- Trust builder: pricing clarity, onboarding confidence, product maturity signals.

## The journey should feel like this
1. Understand the problem.
2. See the product as relevant.
3. Trust the capability.
4. Experience one clear next step.
5. Feel confident moving forward.
