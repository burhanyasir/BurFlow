# BurFlow Product Roadmap

Status: Planning only. No implementation.

## Guiding principle

Every phase should prioritize features that directly help BurFlow customers generate more revenue.

---

## Phase 1 — Revenue-driving engine foundation

### Goals

Establish the core intelligence layer that lets BurFlow act as an AI Website Sales Agent.

### Included capabilities

- Website Scanner
- Knowledge Engine
- Conversion Brain
- AI Sales Widget

### Why this phase matters

These are the core capabilities that make BurFlow useful for lead generation, qualification, and conversion. Without them, the product remains a generic chatbot.

### Success criteria

- A website can be scanned and converted into structured knowledge.
- The system can answer based on retrieved knowledge.
- The assistant can detect buyer intent and select a useful CTA.
- The widget can deliver the experience on third-party sites.

---

## Phase 2 — Usability and operational clarity

### Goals

Make the system easier to adopt and operate.

### Included capabilities

- Onboarding
- Dashboard
- Analytics
- Knowledge Manager

### Why this phase matters

Once the core engine works, the product needs onboarding and visibility to be usable by real customers.

### Success criteria

- New customers can onboard with minimal friction.
- Operators can monitor knowledge health and conversation quality.
- Basic analytics show whether the assistant is converting interest into leads.

---

## Phase 3 — Commercial and team readiness

### Goals

Support broader customer adoption and account management.

### Included capabilities

- Billing
- Teams
- API
- Admin

### Why this phase matters

These capabilities help BurFlow scale from a single-site product to a multi-customer platform.

### Success criteria

- Customers can manage subscriptions and plans.
- Teams can collaborate on the same tenant.
- Admin users can manage access and basic operations.
- API access is available for integrations.

---

## Phase 4 — Enterprise features

### Goals

Support larger organizations with deeper control, governance, and customization.

### Expected capabilities

- advanced governance
- deeper analytics and reporting
- higher-scale deployment support
- enterprise security and compliance controls
- advanced team and workflow management

### Why this phase matters

Enterprise expansion should come after the core product is proven and stable.

---

## Deferred or postponed capabilities

The following should remain postponed until the core revenue-driving engine is validated:

- agency-style proposal and invoice workflows
- broad dashboard polish
- non-core billing experience
- admin-heavy operational features
- team management beyond basic needs

---

## Release principle

BurFlow should ship in a sequence that proves revenue impact before expanding into operational richness.
