# CTA Strategy

## Primary CTA
Scan My Website

### Why
This is the clearest expression of the product promise. It aligns with the customer’s job to be done and creates a concrete action.

## Secondary CTA
Watch Live Demo

### Why
This reduces hesitation for visitors who want proof before they commit.

## Tertiary CTA
Book a Demo / Talk to Sales

### Why
This is best used later in the funnel, after trust has been established.

## CTA guidelines
- Keep one primary CTA per section.
- Use action-oriented, specific language.
- Make buttons visually dominant and consistent.
- Use social and outcome-based language, not abstract AI terms.

## Why this improves conversion
Clear CTA hierarchy minimizes choice overload and helps visitors move forward with confidence.
