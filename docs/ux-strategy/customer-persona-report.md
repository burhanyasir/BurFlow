# Customer Persona Report

## Primary persona: Revenue-focused founder or growth leader
This persona is responsible for growth, demos, or conversion performance. They likely care about pipeline, website conversions, and team productivity.

### Core goals
- Convert more website visitors.
- Reduce manual qualification and onboarding friction.
- Give prospects a faster path to answers and demos.

### Primary fears
- Choosing a tool that feels gimmicky or underperforms.
- Spending time integrating a complex solution.
- Training a chatbot that sounds generic or inaccurate.

### What they need to believe
- BurFlow understands the business quickly.
- The setup is simple and low-risk.
- The widget helps convert rather than distract.

### Buying triggers
- Clear business outcome: more leads, better qualification, better demo conversion.
- Fast proof: the system works on their real site.
- Low friction: no big implementation project.

## Secondary persona: Operations or customer success lead
This persona wants consistency, trust, and oversight.

### Goals
- Create a reliable front-door experience.
- Reduce support load and repetitive answers.

### Concern
- The agent may overpromise or hallucinate.

### Trust builder
- Grounded responses, website-first intelligence, transparent behavior.

## Messaging implications
BurFlow should speak to outcomes, not features. The product should feel like a premium revenue tool with a clear path to ROI.
