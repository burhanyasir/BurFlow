# Product Positioning Document

## Positioning statement
BurFlow is an AI Website Sales Agent that scans a company’s website, understands its business, and helps convert visitors into leads, product matches, and booked demos.

## What BurFlow is not
- Not a generic chatbot
- Not a support-only assistant
- Not a complex enterprise deployment project

## What BurFlow is
- A website-aware sales intelligence layer
- A conversion-focused AI agent
- A fast-to-deploy revenue tool

## Core value props
- Understands the business from the website itself
- Guides visitors toward the right next step
- Helps reduce misqualified traffic and missed conversions
- Feels premium and operationally credible

## Messaging pillars
1. Website-first intelligence
2. Sales conversion focus
3. Fast deployment
4. Grounded and trustworthy responses

## Why this improves conversion
A strong position creates clarity. Clear positioning helps customers understand what the product does, why it matters, and why it is different from generic AI products.
